# 🛣️ Enterprise AI Roadmap (2025-2026)

This document outlines the strategic roadmap for the **PSI Candidate Intelligence Platform**, focusing on transitioning from a single-agent orchestrator to a federated, Test-Time Scaling OS.

## ✅ Completed (Q1-Q2 2026)
*   [x] **LangGraph Swarm Debate**: Recruiter & Tech Lead adversarial reasoning.
*   [x] **WebRTC Proctoring**: OpenCV.js local browser Haar Cascades.
*   [x] **AI Privacy & Identity Layer**: Isolation Forest fraud detection.
*   [x] **Cognitive Interview Engine**: Stateful Web Speech dictation API.
*   [x] **Multi-Source Job Matching**: Remotive, Adzuna, Arbeitnow.
*   [x] **Counterfactual Bias Auditor**: EEOC demographic parity checks.

## 🚀 Near Term (Q3-Q4 2026)
*   [ ] **PSI Foundation Model**: Pre-training a domain-specific LLM purely on parsed resumes and HR tech structures.
*   [ ] **Teacher-Student Distillation**: Fully automating the CRON job that extracts `MLflow` telemetry to train the lightweight `RandomForest` student model.
*   [ ] **RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback)**: Allowing human recruiters to upvote/downvote the Judge Agent's final score to continually fine-tune the LangGraph state.
*   [ ] **GraphRAG Expansion**: Moving from SQLite property graphs to Neo4j.

## 🔮 Long Term (2027+)
*   [ ] **Federated Behavioral Learning**: Instead of tracking biometrics server-side, train lightweight local models in the browser and only sync weight updates (Privacy Preserving AI).
*   [ ] **Large Vision Language Models (LVLM)**: Migrating from traditional OpenCV to Qwen-VL or Llama-Vision to understand body language and physical whiteboarding during the interview.
*   [ ] **ProctorNet**: Developing a proprietary 1M-parameter vision model optimized specifically for multi-face and gaze detection on low-end hardware.
