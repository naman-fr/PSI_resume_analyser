Job Title: UI/UX Designer
Company: Prism Digital Agency
Location: Portland, OR (Hybrid - 3 days in office)

Job Description:
Prism Digital Agency is a full-service digital design and development agency that creates
award-winning digital experiences for clients across technology, healthcare, education, and
non-profit sectors. With a team of 80 creatives, strategists, and technologists, we have
delivered over 300 successful projects for clients ranging from early-stage startups to
established brands like REI, Providence Health, and the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation.
Our work has been recognized by Webby Awards, Awwwards, and Communication Arts.

We are looking for a talented UI/UX Designer to join our Product Design team. You will work on
a diverse portfolio of client projects, designing user interfaces and experiences for web
applications, mobile apps, and e-commerce platforms. This role requires a strong foundation in
user-centered design principles, proficiency with modern design tools, and the ability to
translate complex requirements into intuitive, visually compelling interfaces. You will be
involved in every stage of the design process, from initial research and discovery to final
pixel-perfect handoff to development.

The ideal candidate is a creative problem-solver who is equally comfortable conducting user
research as they are crafting high-fidelity prototypes. You should have a strong visual design
sensibility, an understanding of interaction design patterns, and a portfolio that demonstrates
your ability to solve real user problems with thoughtful design. This is an excellent opportunity
for a designer who thrives on variety and wants to work across multiple industries and project
types.

Requirements:
• 3-5 years of professional UI/UX design experience, preferably at a digital agency or design consultancy
• Expert proficiency with Figma for interface design, prototyping, and design system management
• Strong portfolio demonstrating end-to-end design process from research and wireframes to high-fidelity mockups
• Experience conducting user research methods including user interviews, surveys, usability testing, and card sorting
• Deep understanding of user-centered design (UCD) principles, information architecture, and interaction design
• Experience creating and maintaining scalable design systems and component libraries
• Knowledge of accessibility standards (WCAG 2.1) and inclusive design practices
• Understanding of responsive design principles across desktop, tablet, and mobile breakpoints
• Ability to create interactive prototypes for user testing and stakeholder presentations
• Strong visual design skills including typography, color theory, layout, and iconography
• Excellent communication and presentation skills for client-facing interactions
• Bachelor's degree in Graphic Design, Interaction Design, HCI, or a related field

Nice to Have:
• Experience with motion design and micro-interaction design using tools like Principle, After Effects, or Rive
• Knowledge of front-end development basics (HTML, CSS, JavaScript) for better designer-developer collaboration
• Experience with analytics tools (Hotjar, FullStory, Google Analytics) for data-informed design decisions
• Familiarity with design thinking workshops and facilitation techniques
• Experience designing for e-commerce, SaaS, or healthcare applications
• Knowledge of A/B testing methodologies and conversion rate optimization (CRO)

Responsibilities:
• Lead the UI/UX design process for client projects from discovery and research through delivery and handoff
• Conduct user research including interviews, competitive analysis, heuristic evaluations, and usability testing
• Create user personas, journey maps, information architecture diagrams, and task flows
• Design wireframes, user flows, and interactive prototypes using Figma
• Develop high-fidelity visual designs that align with client brand guidelines and design best practices
• Build and maintain design systems with reusable components, tokens, and documentation
• Present design concepts and rationale to clients, incorporating feedback through iterative design cycles
• Collaborate with frontend and backend developers to ensure accurate implementation of designs
• Ensure all designs meet WCAG 2.1 accessibility standards and are inclusive for diverse user populations
• Participate in design critiques and contribute to the team's collective design quality and knowledge
• Stay current with design trends, tools, and best practices through continuous learning and industry engagement
• Manage design deliverables across multiple concurrent projects while meeting deadlines and quality standards

Benefits:
• Salary range: $85,000 - $120,000 based on experience and portfolio strength
• Annual performance bonus up to 10%
• Health, dental, and vision insurance with employer covering 80% of premiums
• 401(k) with 3% employer match
• 20 days of PTO plus 10 company holidays and the week between Christmas and New Year off
• $1,500 annual creative development stipend for design tools, courses, and conference attendance
• Sabbatical program: 4 weeks of paid sabbatical after 5 years of continuous service
