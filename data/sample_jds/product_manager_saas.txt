Job Title: Product Manager - B2B SaaS Platform
Company: Cloudbridge Software
Location: San Francisco, CA (Hybrid - 3 days in office)

Job Description:
Cloudbridge Software is a venture-backed B2B SaaS company that provides an all-in-one project
management and collaboration platform for mid-market professional services firms. Our platform
is used by over 3,000 companies including consulting firms, marketing agencies, and IT services
providers to manage projects, track time, allocate resources, and invoice clients. We have grown
revenue by 80% year-over-year and recently closed a $75 million Series C round led by Insight
Partners.

We are looking for an experienced Product Manager to own the Resource Management and Capacity
Planning module of our platform. This is one of our highest-value product areas and is critical
to customer retention and expansion. You will be responsible for defining the product vision,
building the roadmap, and driving execution from ideation through launch. You will work in a
cross-functional pod with dedicated engineers, designers, and data analysts.

The ideal candidate has a proven track record of shipping impactful B2B software products, a
deep understanding of Agile development methodologies, and strong analytical skills to make
data-driven decisions. You should be comfortable engaging directly with enterprise customers
to understand their workflows, pain points, and unmet needs. This role reports to the VP of
Product and offers a clear path to senior leadership within the product organization.

Requirements:
• 3-5 years of experience as a Product Manager at a B2B SaaS or enterprise software company
• Demonstrated track record of successfully launching and iterating on software products
• Strong understanding of Agile and Scrum methodologies, including sprint planning, backlog grooming, and retrospectives
• Experience with product analytics tools such as Amplitude, Mixpanel, Pendo, or Google Analytics
• Ability to create detailed product requirements documents (PRDs), user stories, and acceptance criteria
• Experience building and communicating product roadmaps to internal and external stakeholders
• Strong quantitative and analytical skills with the ability to define and track key product metrics (KPIs, OKRs)
• Excellent written and verbal communication skills for executive presentations and customer-facing interactions
• Experience conducting user research, customer interviews, and competitive analysis
• Bachelor's degree in Business, Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field
• Familiarity with basic SQL for querying product data and extracting insights

Nice to Have:
• MBA or advanced degree in a relevant field
• Experience in the professional services, project management, or resource planning domain
• Knowledge of product-led growth (PLG) strategies and freemium/trial conversion optimization
• Experience with product management tools such as Productboard, Aha!, or Jira Product Discovery
• Understanding of API design and technical architecture at a conceptual level
• Previous experience as a software engineer, designer, or consultant before transitioning to product management

Responsibilities:
• Define and own the product vision and roadmap for the Resource Management module, aligned with company strategy
• Conduct ongoing customer discovery through interviews, surveys, and data analysis to identify user needs and pain points
• Write detailed product requirements documents (PRDs) and user stories with clear acceptance criteria
• Prioritize the product backlog using data-driven frameworks (RICE, MoSCoW) and customer impact analysis
• Lead sprint planning, daily standups, and sprint reviews with the cross-functional development pod
• Collaborate with UX designers to create wireframes, prototypes, and validate designs through user testing
• Partner with engineering leads to scope features, estimate effort, and manage technical trade-offs
• Track product metrics and KPIs (adoption rate, time-to-value, NPS, churn) using analytics dashboards
• Communicate product updates, launches, and strategy to leadership, sales, marketing, and customer success teams
• Manage beta programs and early access releases with key customers to gather feedback before general availability
• Conduct competitive analysis and market research to inform product positioning and differentiation
• Present quarterly product strategy and roadmap updates to the executive leadership team

Benefits:
• Base salary range: $135,000 - $170,000 depending on experience
• Annual performance bonus of 15-20%
• Equity compensation: stock options with a 4-year vesting schedule and 1-year cliff
• Comprehensive health, dental, and vision insurance with HSA option
• 401(k) plan with 4% employer match
• Flexible PTO policy with minimum 3 weeks encouraged per year
• Monthly wellness stipend of $150 for gym, mental health, or wellness activities
