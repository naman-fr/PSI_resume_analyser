Job Title: Machine Learning Engineer
Company: Cortex AI Labs
Location: San Francisco, CA (Remote-friendly within the US)

Job Description:
Cortex AI Labs is a cutting-edge artificial intelligence startup focused on building large
language models (LLMs) and generative AI solutions for enterprise applications. Founded by
former researchers from Google DeepMind and Meta AI, our team of 45 engineers and researchers
is pushing the boundaries of what is possible with foundation models. We have raised $60 million
in Series A funding and are working with Fortune 500 clients in legal, finance, and healthcare
to deploy customized AI solutions that automate complex knowledge work.

We are seeking a Machine Learning Engineer to join our Model Deployment and Infrastructure team.
In this role, you will bridge the gap between research and production, taking state-of-the-art
machine learning models developed by our research team and deploying them as scalable, reliable
services that enterprise customers depend on. You will build and maintain the MLOps infrastructure
that enables our team to train, evaluate, version, and serve models efficiently. This includes
working with large language models, fine-tuning pipelines, and inference optimization.

The ideal candidate has strong software engineering skills combined with deep ML knowledge. You
should be comfortable working with GPU clusters, distributed training, model serving frameworks,
and cloud infrastructure. You will work closely with research scientists, backend engineers, and
product teams to ensure our models deliver exceptional performance in production environments.
This is a high-impact role at a company that is at the forefront of the AI revolution.

Requirements:
• 3+ years of experience as a Machine Learning Engineer, ML Infrastructure Engineer, or Applied Scientist
• Strong proficiency in Python and experience with ML frameworks (PyTorch, TensorFlow, JAX)
• Hands-on experience with MLOps tools and practices: experiment tracking (MLflow, Weights & Biases), model versioning, and feature stores
• Experience deploying ML models to production using serving frameworks (TorchServe, TensorFlow Serving, Triton Inference Server, or vLLM)
• Familiarity with large language models (LLMs), transformer architectures, and fine-tuning techniques (LoRA, QLoRA, RLHF)
• Experience with containerization (Docker) and orchestration (Kubernetes) for ML workloads
• Knowledge of cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, or Azure) and GPU compute infrastructure
• Strong software engineering fundamentals: data structures, algorithms, testing, and version control
• Experience with distributed computing frameworks for model training (Horovod, DeepSpeed, or PyTorch FSDP)
• Understanding of model optimization techniques: quantization, pruning, distillation, and ONNX export
• Master's degree or PhD in Computer Science, Machine Learning, or a related field (or equivalent experience)

Nice to Have:
• Experience building Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems and vector databases (Pinecone, Weaviate, Milvus)
• Familiarity with prompt engineering and evaluation frameworks for LLMs
• Experience with streaming inference and real-time ML serving at scale
• Knowledge of data annotation pipelines and human-in-the-loop ML workflows
• Publications at top ML conferences (NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, ACL, EMNLP)
• Experience with multi-modal models (text, image, audio)

Responsibilities:
• Deploy and serve machine learning models including LLMs in production with high availability and low latency
• Build and maintain MLOps pipelines for model training, evaluation, versioning, and monitoring
• Optimize model inference performance through quantization, batching, caching, and hardware-specific optimizations
• Design and implement fine-tuning pipelines for customizing foundation models on client-specific datasets
• Collaborate with research scientists to productionize new model architectures and training techniques
• Build automated evaluation frameworks to measure model quality, safety, and performance across benchmarks
• Manage GPU compute infrastructure and optimize resource utilization for training and inference workloads
• Implement model monitoring and observability to detect data drift, performance degradation, and failure modes
• Develop APIs and SDKs for internal and external consumption of ML model capabilities
• Contribute to the design of our AI platform architecture, including RAG systems and agent frameworks
• Write technical documentation for model deployment procedures, API specifications, and architecture decisions
• Participate in on-call rotations for ML infrastructure and production model serving systems
