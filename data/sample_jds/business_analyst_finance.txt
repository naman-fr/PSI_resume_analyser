Job Title: Business Analyst - Financial Services
Company: Hargrove & Associates Investment Bank
Location: New York, NY (On-site, 5 days per week)

Job Description:
Hargrove & Associates is a mid-tier investment bank with a distinguished 35-year history in
mergers and acquisitions, equity capital markets, and debt advisory services. Headquartered in
Manhattan with offices in London, Hong Kong, and Dubai, we advise corporate clients, private
equity firms, and sovereign wealth funds on transactions ranging from $50 million to $5 billion.
Our firm manages approximately $15 billion in client assets and has been recognized as one of
the top 25 investment banks globally by Institutional Investor.

We are seeking a Business Analyst to join our Strategy and Operations team within the Investment
Banking Division. In this role, you will support senior bankers and managing directors by
conducting financial analysis, building models, preparing client presentations, and generating
data-driven insights that inform strategic decisions. You will work across multiple deal teams
and functional areas, gaining broad exposure to the investment banking business and developing
a strong foundation in financial services.

This role is ideal for a detail-oriented, analytically rigorous professional who thrives in a
fast-paced, high-stakes environment. You will interact regularly with senior stakeholders,
so strong communication and presentation skills are essential. The position offers excellent
opportunities for career advancement, with a clear path to Senior Analyst and Associate roles
based on performance. We value intellectual curiosity, work ethic, and a commitment to excellence.

Requirements:
• 2-4 years of experience as a Business Analyst, Financial Analyst, or similar role in financial services
• Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel including complex financial modeling, pivot tables, VBA macros, and data analysis
• Strong SQL skills for querying databases and extracting data from internal and third-party data sources
• Experience with data visualization and business intelligence tools, preferably Tableau or Power BI
• Solid understanding of financial statements (income statement, balance sheet, cash flow) and key financial metrics
• Experience creating polished executive presentations and client deliverables using Microsoft PowerPoint
• Strong quantitative and analytical skills with attention to detail and accuracy
• Knowledge of valuation methodologies including DCF, comparable company analysis, and precedent transactions
• Excellent written and verbal communication skills for stakeholder presentations and report writing
• Bachelor's degree in Finance, Economics, Accounting, Business Administration, or a related field
• Ability to manage multiple workstreams simultaneously and deliver under tight deadlines

Nice to Have:
• CFA Level I or II candidate, or completion of the CFA program
• Experience with Bloomberg Terminal, Capital IQ, FactSet, or PitchBook for financial data research
• Knowledge of Python or R for data analysis and automation of recurring analytical workflows
• MBA or master's degree in a quantitative or business discipline
• Prior experience in investment banking, private equity, or management consulting
• Familiarity with regulatory reporting requirements (SEC filings, Basel III, Dodd-Frank)

Responsibilities:
• Build and maintain detailed financial models including DCF, LBO, merger, and accretion/dilution analyses
• Prepare client-ready pitch books, information memoranda, and management presentations using PowerPoint
• Conduct industry research and competitive analysis to support deal origination and client advisory engagements
• Extract, clean, and analyze data from internal databases, Bloomberg, Capital IQ, and other financial data providers
• Create and maintain Tableau dashboards to track deal pipeline, revenue metrics, and operational KPIs
• Support due diligence processes by compiling and analyzing financial and operational data for potential transactions
• Collaborate with deal teams across M&A, equity capital markets, and debt advisory on active transactions
• Automate recurring reporting workflows using Excel VBA and SQL to improve efficiency and reduce manual effort
• Present analytical findings and recommendations to senior bankers and managing directors
• Monitor market trends, economic indicators, and regulatory developments relevant to client sectors
• Maintain and update the firm's internal deal database and CRM system with accurate transaction information
• Assist in the preparation of quarterly business reviews and strategic planning materials for senior leadership

Benefits:
• Base salary range: $90,000 - $130,000 depending on experience and qualifications
• Annual discretionary bonus of 20-50% of base salary based on individual and firm performance
• Comprehensive health, dental, and vision insurance
• 401(k) with 5% employer match
• 20 days of PTO plus 10 bank holidays
• Sponsorship for CFA exam fees and study materials
• Subsidized commuter benefits and parking
• Access to firm-sponsored networking events and industry conferences
