Job Title: Senior Frontend Developer - React
Company: Meridian Financial Technologies
Location: New York, NY (Hybrid - 2 days in office)

Job Description:
Meridian Financial Technologies is a leading fintech company that provides institutional-grade
trading platforms and portfolio management tools to hedge funds, asset managers, and family offices
worldwide. Our platform handles over $50 billion in assets under management and supports
real-time trading across equities, fixed income, derivatives, and cryptocurrency markets. We are
a profitable, rapidly growing company with over 300 employees and offices in New York, London,
and Singapore.

We are seeking an experienced Senior Frontend Developer to join our Trading Experience team. You
will lead the development of our next-generation trading dashboard, a high-performance web
application that enables traders to execute complex strategies with millisecond-level responsiveness.
This role demands deep expertise in React, TypeScript, and modern frontend architecture, as well
as a passion for building performant, accessible, and visually polished user interfaces in a
data-intensive domain.

As a senior member of the team, you will be expected to set technical standards, drive architectural
decisions, and mentor mid-level and junior developers. You will collaborate closely with backend
engineers, UX designers, quantitative analysts, and product stakeholders to deliver features that
directly impact our clients' trading operations. This is a high-impact role where your work will
be used daily by professional traders managing billions of dollars.

Requirements:
• 5+ years of professional frontend development experience with React and the modern JavaScript ecosystem
• Expert-level proficiency in TypeScript, including advanced types, generics, and utility types
• Strong experience with Next.js for server-side rendering, static generation, and API routes
• Hands-on experience with GraphQL (Apollo Client or Relay) for data fetching and state management
• Deep understanding of React performance optimization techniques (memoization, virtualization, code splitting)
• Experience building complex, data-heavy UIs such as dashboards, tables, charts, and real-time feeds
• Proficiency with modern CSS solutions including CSS Modules, Tailwind CSS, or styled-components
• Strong experience with state management libraries (Redux Toolkit, Zustand, or Recoil)
• Familiarity with testing frameworks such as Jest, React Testing Library, and Cypress
• Experience with WebSocket connections for real-time data streaming
• Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or equivalent professional experience
• Excellent communication skills and experience working in cross-functional teams

Nice to Have:
• Experience in the fintech, trading, or financial services industry
• Knowledge of data visualization libraries such as D3.js, Highcharts, or Recharts
• Experience with micro-frontend architecture and module federation
• Familiarity with accessibility standards (WCAG 2.1) and inclusive design principles
• Understanding of financial instruments, market data feeds, and FIX protocol
• Experience with design systems and component library development (Storybook)

Responsibilities:
• Lead the frontend architecture and development of our real-time trading dashboard application
• Build reusable, well-documented React component libraries that follow our design system
• Optimize application performance to achieve sub-100ms rendering for complex data grids and charts
• Collaborate with UX designers to translate wireframes and prototypes into pixel-perfect implementations
• Integrate with GraphQL APIs and WebSocket streams to display real-time market data and order updates
• Write comprehensive unit tests, integration tests, and end-to-end tests to maintain high code quality
• Conduct code reviews and establish frontend coding standards, best practices, and style guides
• Mentor and coach junior and mid-level frontend developers on the team
• Participate in sprint planning, backlog grooming, and retrospectives as part of the Agile development process
• Evaluate and introduce new frontend tools, libraries, and technologies to improve developer productivity
• Work with the DevOps team to optimize CI/CD pipelines for frontend builds and deployments
• Contribute to technical documentation including architecture decision records (ADRs) and API integration guides

• Annual performance bonus: 15-25% of base salary
• Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) with a 4-year vesting schedule
• Comprehensive medical, dental, and vision insurance with low employee contributions
• 401(k) with 6% company match, fully vested from day one
• 25 days of paid time off plus 10 company holidays
• Commuter benefits and subsidized gym membership
