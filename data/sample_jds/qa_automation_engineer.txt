Job Title: QA Automation Engineer
Company: Lumenwork SaaS Solutions
Location: Denver, CO (Remote-first)

Job Description:
Lumenwork SaaS Solutions provides a cloud-based workforce management platform used by over
2,000 mid-market and enterprise companies to manage employee scheduling, time tracking, payroll
integration, and compliance reporting. Our platform serves industries including healthcare,
hospitality, retail, and manufacturing, handling the scheduling needs of over 5 million workers.
We are a profitable, bootstrapped company with 200 employees and a strong culture of engineering
excellence and quality.

We are looking for a QA Automation Engineer to join our Quality Engineering team and help us
build and scale our automated testing infrastructure. In this role, you will design, develop,
and maintain automated test suites across our web application, REST APIs, and backend services.
You will work closely with development teams to embed quality practices into every stage of the
software development lifecycle, from feature design through production deployment. Our goal is
to achieve comprehensive automated test coverage that enables confident, rapid releases.

The ideal candidate has deep experience with test automation frameworks, a strong understanding
of software testing principles, and the ability to think critically about edge cases and failure
modes. You should be comfortable writing code in Python or JavaScript, working with CI/CD
pipelines, and advocating for quality best practices across the engineering organization. This
role offers the opportunity to shape our testing strategy and have a direct impact on the
reliability and quality of our product.

Requirements:
• 3-5 years of professional experience in QA engineering with a strong focus on test automation
• Proficiency with Selenium WebDriver or Playwright for web UI test automation
• Experience with Cypress for end-to-end testing of modern web applications
• Strong programming skills in Python (pytest) or JavaScript/TypeScript for writing test scripts and frameworks
• Experience with API testing using tools such as Postman, REST Assured, or requests library
• Hands-on experience integrating automated tests into CI/CD pipelines (Jenkins, GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, or CircleCI)
• Solid understanding of software testing methodologies: functional, regression, integration, performance, and smoke testing
• Experience with test management tools such as TestRail, Zephyr, or Xray
• Knowledge of version control systems (Git) and collaborative development workflows
• Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with attention to detail
• Excellent communication skills for reporting bugs, test results, and quality metrics to stakeholders
• Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field

Nice to Have:
• Experience with performance testing tools such as JMeter, k6, or Locust
• Knowledge of mobile testing frameworks (Appium, XCUITest, Espresso)
• Experience with contract testing using Pact or similar frameworks
• Familiarity with Docker and Kubernetes for managing test environments
• ISTQB certification (Foundation or Advanced level)
• Experience with behavior-driven development (BDD) using Cucumber or Behave

Responsibilities:
• Design, develop, and maintain automated test suites for web UI, APIs, and backend services
• Create and execute comprehensive test plans and test cases for new features and regression testing
• Integrate automated tests into CI/CD pipelines to enable continuous testing on every code commit
• Identify, document, and track bugs with clear reproduction steps, severity, and expected vs. actual behavior
• Collaborate with developers during feature development to define testability requirements and acceptance criteria
• Develop and maintain test data management strategies and test environment configurations
• Perform manual exploratory testing for complex features and edge cases that are difficult to automate
• Create and maintain API test suites to validate endpoint functionality, error handling, and data integrity
• Monitor test execution results, analyze flaky tests, and improve test reliability and stability
• Generate and present quality metrics and test coverage reports to engineering leadership
• Participate in sprint planning and backlog grooming to estimate testing effort for upcoming features
• Contribute to the team's QA best practices, testing standards, and knowledge base documentation

Benefits:
• Salary range: $100,000 - $140,000 based on experience
• Annual performance bonus of 8-12%
• Health, dental, and vision insurance with employer covering 85% of premiums
• 401(k) with 4% company match
• Remote work stipend of $100 per month for internet and home office expenses
• 4 weeks of PTO plus 10 company holidays
• $2,000 annual professional development budget for certifications, courses, and conferences
• Quarterly company retreats at various locations across the US
