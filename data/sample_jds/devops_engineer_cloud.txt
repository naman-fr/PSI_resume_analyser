Job Title: DevOps Engineer - Cloud Infrastructure
Company: Axiom Enterprise Solutions
Location: Seattle, WA (Remote-friendly)

Job Description:
Axiom Enterprise Solutions is a global enterprise software company providing mission-critical
ERP and CRM solutions to Fortune 1000 companies. With over 5,000 employees across 20 countries,
we serve clients in manufacturing, retail, and financial services industries. Our cloud platform
processes billions of API requests daily and maintains 99.99% uptime SLAs for our enterprise
customers. We are in the midst of a large-scale cloud migration initiative, moving our legacy
on-premise infrastructure to a fully cloud-native architecture on AWS.

We are seeking a skilled DevOps Engineer to join our Cloud Infrastructure team. You will play a
critical role in designing, building, and maintaining the cloud infrastructure that supports our
SaaS platform serving thousands of enterprise clients. This position requires deep hands-on
experience with AWS services, container orchestration with Kubernetes, infrastructure as code
with Terraform, and CI/CD pipeline design and optimization.

You will work in a fast-paced environment alongside software engineers, site reliability engineers,
and security teams to ensure our infrastructure is scalable, resilient, and cost-efficient. The
ideal candidate is someone who thrives on automation, has a strong security mindset, and is
passionate about building reliable systems at scale. You will have significant influence over
our infrastructure strategy and will be instrumental in shaping the future of our cloud platform.

Requirements:
• 4+ years of professional experience in DevOps, Cloud Engineering, or Site Reliability Engineering
• Expert-level knowledge of AWS services including EC2, ECS, EKS, Lambda, S3, RDS, CloudFront, IAM, and VPC
• Strong hands-on experience with Kubernetes for container orchestration (deployment, scaling, monitoring, troubleshooting)
• Proficiency with Terraform for infrastructure as code (modules, workspaces, state management, drift detection)
• Experience designing and maintaining CI/CD pipelines using tools such as Jenkins, GitLab CI, GitHub Actions, or CircleCI
• Strong scripting skills in Bash, Python, or Go for automation tasks
• Deep understanding of networking concepts including DNS, load balancing, VPNs, firewalls, and TLS/SSL
• Experience with monitoring and observability tools such as Prometheus, Grafana, CloudWatch, or Datadog
• Knowledge of security best practices including secrets management (Vault, AWS Secrets Manager), IAM policies, and network segmentation
• Experience with configuration management tools such as Ansible, Chef, or Puppet
• Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or equivalent experience

Nice to Have:
• AWS certifications such as Solutions Architect Professional or DevOps Engineer Professional
• Experience with service mesh technologies (Istio, Linkerd)
• Knowledge of GitOps practices and tools (ArgoCD, Flux)
• Experience with cost optimization strategies and FinOps principles
• Familiarity with compliance frameworks such as SOC 2, ISO 27001, or PCI DSS
• Experience with multi-region and disaster recovery architecture design

Responsibilities:
• Design, provision, and manage AWS cloud infrastructure using Terraform and CloudFormation
• Build and maintain Kubernetes clusters for running containerized microservices at scale
• Design and optimize CI/CD pipelines to enable fast, reliable, and secure software deployments
• Implement infrastructure monitoring, alerting, and logging using Prometheus, Grafana, and ELK Stack
• Automate operational tasks including server provisioning, configuration management, and secret rotation
• Collaborate with development teams to containerize applications and define Kubernetes deployment manifests
• Conduct capacity planning and implement auto-scaling strategies to handle variable workloads
• Perform security hardening of cloud infrastructure and ensure compliance with SOC 2 and ISO 27001 standards
• Manage DNS, CDN, load balancer, and SSL certificate configurations for production services
• Participate in incident response and post-mortem processes to improve system reliability
• Maintain documentation for infrastructure architecture, runbooks, and disaster recovery procedures
• Evaluate and recommend new tools and technologies to improve infrastructure efficiency and developer experience

• Annual bonus of 10-15% tied to company and individual performance
• Stock purchase plan with 15% discount on company shares
• Full medical, dental, and vision coverage for employees and dependents
• 401(k) with dollar-for-dollar match up to 6% of salary
• 4 weeks of PTO plus 11 company holidays
• $3,000 annual professional development budget for certifications and conferences
