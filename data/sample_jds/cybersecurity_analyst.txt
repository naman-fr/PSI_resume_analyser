Job Title: Cybersecurity Analyst
Company: Pinnacle National Bank
Location: Charlotte, NC (On-site)

Job Description:
Pinnacle National Bank is one of the largest regional banks in the southeastern United States,
with over $80 billion in total assets, 1,200 branch locations, and 15,000 employees. We serve
millions of retail and commercial banking customers and are committed to maintaining the highest
standards of security and trust. Our Information Security division is responsible for protecting
customer data, financial systems, and digital banking infrastructure from an ever-evolving
landscape of cyber threats.

We are hiring a Cybersecurity Analyst to join our Security Operations Center (SOC). In this role,
you will monitor, detect, analyze, and respond to cybersecurity threats targeting our banking
infrastructure and customer-facing applications. You will work with enterprise-grade security
tools including SIEM platforms, endpoint detection and response (EDR) solutions, and threat
intelligence feeds to identify and mitigate security incidents in real time. This is a hands-on
technical role that requires vigilance, analytical thinking, and the ability to act quickly under
pressure.

The ideal candidate has a solid foundation in cybersecurity principles, experience with security
monitoring and incident response, and a strong understanding of the regulatory landscape in
financial services. You will be part of a team of 12 security professionals and will report to
the SOC Manager. This position requires working in rotating shifts to ensure 24/7 security
coverage, including occasional nights and weekends.

Requirements:
• 3+ years of professional experience in cybersecurity, information security, or security operations
• Hands-on experience with SIEM platforms such as Splunk, IBM QRadar, or Microsoft Sentinel
• Strong knowledge of incident response procedures, including detection, containment, eradication, and recovery
• Experience with endpoint detection and response (EDR) tools such as CrowdStrike Falcon, Carbon Black, or SentinelOne
• Understanding of network security concepts including firewalls, IDS/IPS, VPNs, and network segmentation
• Familiarity with common attack frameworks (MITRE ATT&CK) and threat intelligence analysis
• Knowledge of regulatory compliance frameworks relevant to banking: PCI DSS, SOX, GLBA, FFIEC
• Experience with vulnerability scanning tools such as Nessus, Qualys, or Rapid7
• Strong understanding of TCP/IP networking, DNS, HTTP/HTTPS, and common network protocols
• Bachelor's degree in Cybersecurity, Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field
• One or more professional certifications: CISSP, CompTIA Security+, CEH, GIAC (GSEC, GCIH, or GCIA)

Nice to Have:
• Experience working in a financial services or banking SOC environment
• Familiarity with digital forensics tools and techniques (EnCase, FTK, Volatility)
• Knowledge of cloud security in AWS, Azure, or GCP environments
• Experience with security automation and orchestration (SOAR) platforms
• Understanding of identity and access management (IAM) solutions and privileged access management
• Experience with penetration testing or red team exercises

Responsibilities:
• Monitor security alerts and events from SIEM, EDR, and other security tools on a continuous basis
• Investigate and triage security incidents, determining severity, scope, and root cause
• Execute incident response playbooks for containment, eradication, and recovery of security incidents
• Analyze network traffic, system logs, and endpoint telemetry to detect indicators of compromise (IoCs)
• Conduct threat hunting activities to proactively identify advanced persistent threats (APTs) and insider threats
• Maintain and tune SIEM correlation rules, alerts, and dashboards to reduce false positives
• Produce detailed incident reports and post-incident reviews for management and compliance teams
• Collaborate with IT operations, network engineering, and application teams on remediation efforts
• Perform regular vulnerability assessments and coordinate patching activities with system administrators
• Stay current with emerging cyber threats, vulnerabilities, and attack techniques through threat intelligence feeds
• Support internal and external audits by providing security documentation and evidence
• Participate in tabletop exercises and incident response drills to improve team readiness

• Comprehensive medical, dental, and vision insurance for employees and families
• Pension plan with 7% employer contribution
• 401(k) with 5% company match
• 22 days of PTO plus 11 bank holidays
• Tuition reimbursement up to $8,000 per year for relevant degree programs or certifications
• Shift differential pay for evening and weekend rotations
