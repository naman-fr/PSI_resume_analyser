Job Title: Mobile Developer - iOS & Android
Company: VibeCheck Social Inc.
Location: Los Angeles, CA (Hybrid - 2 days in office)

Job Description:
VibeCheck Social is a fast-growing consumer social media company that connects people through
short-form video content and real-time interactive experiences. Our flagship app has over 25
million monthly active users and is consistently ranked among the top 50 social networking apps
on both the App Store and Google Play. We have raised $120 million in funding to date and are
backed by premier investors including Benchmark, Lightspeed Venture Partners, and SV Angel.

We are looking for a talented Mobile Developer to join our Core Experience team. You will work
on building and enhancing the features that millions of users interact with daily, including
video creation tools, real-time messaging, social feeds, push notifications, and in-app
purchases. This role requires experience with both iOS (Swift) and Android (Kotlin) development,
as well as cross-platform frameworks like Flutter. You should be passionate about mobile user
experiences and motivated by building products that people love.

As part of a team of 10 mobile engineers, you will collaborate closely with backend engineers,
product designers, data scientists, and content moderation specialists. We operate in a
fast-paced, experiment-driven culture where we ship multiple app updates per week and
continuously optimize based on user data. The ideal candidate is someone who takes pride in
writing clean, performant code and is excited about the unique challenges of building a social
platform at scale.

Requirements:
• 3+ years of professional mobile development experience shipping consumer-facing applications
• Proficiency in Swift for native iOS development, including UIKit and SwiftUI
• Proficiency in Kotlin for native Android development, including Jetpack Compose and Android Architecture Components
• Experience with Flutter or React Native for cross-platform mobile development
• Strong understanding of mobile app architecture patterns (MVVM, MVI, Clean Architecture)
• Experience with RESTful APIs and real-time communication protocols (WebSockets, MQTT)
• Familiarity with mobile CI/CD pipelines and automated testing frameworks (XCTest, Espresso, Appium)
• Experience with app store submission processes for both Apple App Store and Google Play Store
• Understanding of mobile performance optimization including memory management, battery consumption, and network efficiency
• Knowledge of mobile analytics and crash reporting tools (Firebase, Crashlytics, Mixpanel)
• Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or equivalent experience

Nice to Have:
• Experience with video processing, encoding, and playback on mobile (AVFoundation, ExoPlayer)
• Knowledge of augmented reality frameworks (ARKit, ARCore) or camera SDKs
• Experience with push notification services (APNs, FCM) and deep linking
• Familiarity with in-app purchase and subscription management (StoreKit, Google Play Billing)
• Experience with accessibility features and inclusive mobile design
• Knowledge of GraphQL for mobile API consumption

Responsibilities:
• Design, develop, and ship new features for our iOS and Android applications
• Build smooth, performant UI components using SwiftUI, Jetpack Compose, and Flutter widgets
• Integrate mobile apps with backend services through RESTful APIs and WebSocket connections
• Optimize app performance including startup time, frame rate, memory usage, and network requests
• Write unit tests, UI tests, and integration tests to maintain high code quality and prevent regressions
• Collaborate with UX designers to implement pixel-perfect designs and micro-interactions
• Participate in A/B testing and feature flagging to validate new features with user segments
• Monitor app health using crash reporting and analytics dashboards, and address top crashers promptly
• Manage app releases through TestFlight (iOS) and Google Play Beta tracks for staged rollouts
• Contribute to shared mobile libraries and SDKs used across multiple apps and features
• Stay current with the latest iOS and Android platform updates, APIs, and design guidelines
• Participate in code reviews, architecture discussions, and sprint planning ceremonies
