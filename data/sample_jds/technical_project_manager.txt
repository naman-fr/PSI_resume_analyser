Job Title: Technical Project Manager
Company: Stratos Consulting Group
Location: Washington, DC (Hybrid - 3 days in office, client site as needed)

Job Description:
Stratos Consulting Group is a technology consulting firm specializing in digital transformation,
cloud migration, and custom software development for federal government agencies and large
commercial enterprises. With over 600 consultants and offices in Washington DC, Atlanta, and
Reston, Virginia, we have been delivering complex technology projects for over 15 years. Our
clients include the Department of Defense, Department of Homeland Security, NASA, and Fortune
500 companies in the defense, healthcare, and financial services sectors.

We are seeking an experienced Technical Project Manager to lead complex, multi-workstream
technology projects for our government and commercial clients. In this role, you will be
responsible for the end-to-end management of software development and infrastructure projects,
from initial scoping and planning through delivery and post-deployment support. You will
manage cross-functional teams of 10-25 engineers, designers, and analysts, ensuring projects
are delivered on time, within budget, and to the highest quality standards.

The ideal candidate has a proven track record of managing technical projects in a consulting
or professional services environment. You should be deeply familiar with Agile and Scrum
methodologies, have excellent stakeholder management skills, and be comfortable navigating
the complexities of government contracting or large enterprise engagements. This role requires
a blend of technical understanding, leadership ability, and business acumen. Strong candidates
will have PMP or CSM certification and experience managing projects with budgets exceeding
$2 million.

Requirements:
• 5-8 years of experience managing technology projects, preferably in a consulting or professional services firm
• Deep expertise in Agile/Scrum methodologies, including experience as a Scrum Master or Agile Coach
• Proficiency with project management tools: JIRA, Confluence, Microsoft Project, or Smartsheet
• Proven track record of delivering complex software development projects on time and within budget
• Strong stakeholder management skills with experience interfacing with C-level executives and government officials
• Experience managing project budgets, forecasts, resource allocation, and profitability
• Understanding of software development lifecycle (SDLC) and ability to communicate effectively with technical teams
• Experience managing distributed and cross-functional teams of 10+ people across multiple workstreams
• PMP (Project Management Professional) or CSM (Certified Scrum Master) certification
• Strong risk identification, mitigation planning, and issue resolution capabilities
• Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including experience with executive-level reporting
• Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Business, or a related field

Nice to Have:
• Experience with federal government contracting, including FAR/DFAR regulations and security clearances
• SAFe (Scaled Agile Framework) certification or experience implementing SAFe in large organizations
• Technical background in software development, cloud infrastructure, or data engineering
• Experience with DevOps practices and CI/CD pipeline management
• Knowledge of ITIL framework for IT service management
• Active Secret or Top Secret security clearance (or ability to obtain one)

Responsibilities:
• Lead end-to-end project delivery for complex technology engagements from initiation through closure
• Define project scope, objectives, deliverables, and success criteria in collaboration with clients and stakeholders
• Develop and maintain detailed project plans, schedules, and work breakdown structures (WBS)
• Facilitate Agile ceremonies including sprint planning, daily standups, sprint reviews, and retrospectives
• Manage project budgets, track actuals vs. forecasts, and report financial status to leadership
• Identify, assess, and mitigate project risks and issues, escalating to senior leadership when necessary
• Coordinate cross-functional teams including developers, QA engineers, UX designers, and DevOps engineers
• Serve as the primary point of contact for client stakeholders, maintaining regular communication and managing expectations
• Prepare and deliver project status reports, dashboards, and executive briefings for internal and client leadership
• Manage resource allocation and capacity planning across multiple concurrent projects
• Ensure project deliverables meet quality standards through regular reviews and acceptance testing
• Drive continuous improvement by conducting project retrospectives and implementing lessons learned

Benefits:
• Salary range: $120,000 - $165,000 depending on experience and certifications
• Annual performance bonus of 10-20% based on project delivery and client satisfaction
• Comprehensive health, dental, and vision insurance for employees and dependents
• 401(k) with 5% employer match, fully vested after 2 years
• 25 days of PTO plus 11 federal holidays
• $4,000 annual professional development budget for certifications, training, and conferences
• Company-paid PMP, CSM, and SAFe certification exams and renewal fees
• Monthly cell phone and internet reimbursement
