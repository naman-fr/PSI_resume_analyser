Job Title: Data Scientist - Machine Learning
Company: Veridian Health Analytics
Location: Boston, MA (Hybrid - 3 days in office)

Job Description:
Veridian Health Analytics is a healthcare technology company dedicated to improving patient outcomes
through advanced data science and machine learning. We partner with leading hospital systems,
pharmaceutical companies, and health insurance providers to build predictive models that identify
at-risk patients, optimize treatment plans, and reduce healthcare costs. Our platform currently
serves over 40 million patient records across 200+ healthcare facilities in the United States.

We are looking for a Data Scientist with a strong machine learning background to join our Clinical
Insights team. In this role, you will develop and deploy predictive models that power clinical
decision support tools used by doctors and nurses at the point of care. You will work with
large-scale electronic health record (EHR) data, claims data, and genomic data to uncover
patterns that lead to better patient outcomes. Our team operates at the intersection of
cutting-edge AI research and real-world clinical impact.

The ideal candidate combines strong statistical foundations with practical machine learning
engineering skills. You should be comfortable working with messy, real-world healthcare data,
navigating regulatory requirements such as HIPAA, and communicating complex technical findings
to non-technical stakeholders including clinicians and hospital administrators. This role offers
the rare opportunity to apply your data science expertise to problems that genuinely save lives.

Requirements:
• 3+ years of professional experience as a Data Scientist, Machine Learning Engineer, or similar role
• Strong proficiency in Python for data analysis and machine learning (NumPy, pandas, scikit-learn)
• Hands-on experience with deep learning frameworks such as TensorFlow or PyTorch
• Solid foundation in statistics, probability, and experimental design (A/B testing, hypothesis testing)
• Proficiency in SQL for querying large datasets from relational databases and data warehouses
• Experience with the full machine learning lifecycle: data collection, feature engineering, model training, evaluation, and deployment
• Familiarity with data visualization tools and libraries (Matplotlib, Seaborn, Plotly, or Tableau)
• Experience working with large-scale, structured and unstructured datasets (1M+ records)
• Master's degree or PhD in Computer Science, Statistics, Mathematics, Biostatistics, or a related quantitative field
• Strong communication skills with the ability to present findings to both technical and non-technical audiences
• Understanding of model interpretability and explainability techniques (SHAP, LIME)

Nice to Have:
• Experience working with healthcare data such as EHR, claims, or clinical trial data
• Knowledge of HIPAA regulations and data privacy best practices in healthcare
• Experience with natural language processing (NLP) for clinical text mining
• Familiarity with cloud-based ML platforms (AWS SageMaker, Google Vertex AI, or Azure ML)
• Publications in peer-reviewed journals or presentations at ML/AI conferences
• Experience with time-series analysis and survival analysis methods

Responsibilities:
• Develop and validate predictive models for clinical risk stratification and patient outcome prediction
• Perform exploratory data analysis on large-scale healthcare datasets to identify trends and insights
• Engineer features from raw EHR data, claims data, and laboratory results for model development
• Collaborate with clinical advisors and domain experts to ensure models are clinically relevant and actionable
• Design and conduct A/B tests and statistical experiments to measure the impact of model-driven interventions
• Build automated data pipelines for model training, validation, and monitoring using Python and SQL
• Present research findings and model performance metrics to stakeholders through reports and presentations
• Ensure all data handling and model development complies with HIPAA and other healthcare regulations
• Contribute to the team's research agenda by staying current with the latest ML and healthcare AI literature
• Work with ML engineers to deploy models into production systems and monitor their real-world performance
• Document methodologies, assumptions, and results in technical reports and internal knowledge bases

Benefits:
• Salary range: $130,000 - $170,000 based on experience and qualifications
• Annual performance bonus of 10-20% based on individual and company performance
• Comprehensive health insurance including medical, dental, vision, and mental health coverage
• 401(k) retirement plan with 5% employer match
• 20 days PTO plus 12 company holidays and 5 sick days
• Tuition reimbursement program up to $10,000 per year for continuing education
• Flexible work schedule with the option to work remotely 2 days per week
