Job Title: Data Engineer - ETL & Data Pipelines
Company: TransGlobal Logistics Corp.
Location: Dallas, TX (Hybrid - 3 days in office)

Job Description:
TransGlobal Logistics Corp. is one of the largest freight and supply chain management companies
in North America, operating a network of over 500 distribution centers and managing the movement
of goods for Fortune 500 retailers, manufacturers, and e-commerce companies. Our operations
generate terabytes of data daily from GPS tracking, warehouse management systems, route
optimization engines, and customer order platforms. Data is at the heart of our competitive
advantage, enabling us to optimize delivery routes, predict demand, and reduce operational costs.

We are seeking a Data Engineer to join our Enterprise Data Platform team. You will be responsible
for designing, building, and maintaining the data pipelines and infrastructure that power our
analytics, reporting, and machine learning capabilities. You will work with massive datasets
from diverse sources including IoT sensors, transactional databases, third-party APIs, and
streaming event platforms. Your work will directly impact decisions made by operations managers,
route planners, and executive leadership across the organization.

The ideal candidate has strong experience with modern data engineering tools and practices,
including batch and streaming ETL pipelines, cloud data warehouses, and data orchestration
frameworks. You should be comfortable working with both structured and semi-structured data,
and you should have a passion for building reliable, scalable data systems. This role offers
the opportunity to work on high-impact data problems in an industry undergoing rapid digital
transformation.

Requirements:
• 4+ years of professional experience as a Data Engineer, Analytics Engineer, or similar data-focused role
• Strong proficiency in Python and SQL for data processing, transformation, and pipeline development
• Hands-on experience with Apache Spark (PySpark or Spark SQL) for large-scale data processing
• Experience with Apache Airflow or similar workflow orchestration tools (Prefect, Dagster, Luigi)
• Proficiency with Snowflake, BigQuery, or Redshift for cloud data warehousing
• Experience with dbt (data build tool) for data transformation and modeling
• Knowledge of data modeling concepts including star schema, snowflake schema, and data vault methodology
• Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, or Azure) and related data services (S3, Glue, Kinesis, Pub/Sub)
• Familiarity with version control (Git) and CI/CD for data pipelines
• Understanding of data quality frameworks and data governance best practices
• Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Data Engineering, Information Systems, or a related field

Nice to Have:
• Experience with real-time streaming data using Apache Kafka, AWS Kinesis, or Google Pub/Sub
• Knowledge of Delta Lake, Apache Iceberg, or Apache Hudi for lakehouse architecture
• Familiarity with containerization (Docker) and orchestration (Kubernetes) for data workloads
• Experience with data cataloging and metadata management tools (Alation, Collibra, DataHub)
• Understanding of logistics, supply chain, or transportation industry data
• Experience with Great Expectations or similar data quality testing frameworks

Responsibilities:
• Design, build, and maintain scalable ETL/ELT pipelines using Python, Spark, and Airflow
• Develop and optimize data models in Snowflake using dbt for analytics and reporting use cases
• Ingest data from diverse sources including relational databases, REST APIs, flat files, and streaming platforms
• Implement data quality checks and monitoring to ensure accuracy, completeness, and timeliness of data
• Collaborate with data scientists and analysts to understand data requirements and deliver reliable datasets
• Build and maintain data lake and data warehouse infrastructure on AWS (S3, Glue, Athena, Redshift)
• Optimize query performance and storage costs in the cloud data warehouse through partitioning, clustering, and materialized views
• Create and maintain documentation for data pipelines, schemas, and data dictionaries
• Participate in data governance initiatives including data cataloging, lineage tracking, and access control
• Troubleshoot and resolve data pipeline failures, ensuring SLAs for data freshness are consistently met
• Support the migration of legacy data systems to modern cloud-native architectures
• Mentor junior data engineers and conduct code reviews for data pipeline code

• 3 weeks of PTO plus 10 company holidays
