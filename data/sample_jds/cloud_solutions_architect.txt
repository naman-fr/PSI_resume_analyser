Job Title: Cloud Solutions Architect
Company: Vanguard Industrial Technologies
Location: Houston, TX (Hybrid - 3 days in office, travel up to 15%)

Job Description:
Vanguard Industrial Technologies is a Fortune 500 diversified industrial conglomerate with
annual revenues exceeding $35 billion. We operate across four major business segments: energy
infrastructure, advanced manufacturing, aerospace components, and industrial automation. With
over 80,000 employees in 45 countries, we are a global leader in industrial innovation. Our
IT organization is undergoing a multi-year digital transformation initiative to modernize our
technology landscape, migrate to the cloud, and enable data-driven decision making across all
business segments.

We are seeking a Cloud Solutions Architect to join our Enterprise Architecture team within our
Corporate IT division. In this senior-level role, you will be responsible for designing
enterprise-grade cloud architectures that support our business-critical applications, data
platforms, and digital transformation initiatives. You will work across all three major cloud
providers (AWS, Azure, and GCP) and will be expected to make strategic recommendations on cloud
adoption, migration strategies, and multi-cloud governance. Your designs must meet stringent
requirements for scalability, reliability, security, and regulatory compliance.

This is a highly visible, strategic role where you will collaborate with business unit CIOs,
VP-level stakeholders, application development teams, cybersecurity, and vendor partners. The
ideal candidate has broad and deep experience in cloud architecture, a strong understanding of
enterprise systems integration, and the ability to translate complex technical concepts into
business-friendly language. You should be a thought leader who can influence technology strategy
at the highest levels of the organization while also being hands-on enough to create detailed
architecture diagrams and proof-of-concept implementations.

Requirements:
• 8+ years of experience in IT architecture, with at least 5 years focused on cloud solutions architecture
• Expert-level knowledge of at least one major cloud platform (AWS, Azure, or GCP) with working knowledge of the other two
• Strong experience designing microservices architectures using containers, Kubernetes, and serverless technologies
• Deep understanding of system design principles: scalability, high availability, fault tolerance, and disaster recovery
• Experience with cloud migration strategies (6 Rs: rehost, replatform, refactor, repurchase, retire, retain)
• Knowledge of enterprise integration patterns, API management (Apigee, Kong, AWS API Gateway), and event-driven architectures
• Experience with infrastructure as code (Terraform, CloudFormation, Bicep) and GitOps practices
• Strong understanding of cloud security architecture: identity federation, zero-trust networking, encryption, and compliance
• Experience with data architecture including data lakes, data warehouses, streaming platforms, and analytics solutions
• Excellent communication and presentation skills for engaging with executive stakeholders and technical teams
• Relevant cloud certifications: AWS Solutions Architect Professional, Azure Solutions Architect Expert, or GCP Professional Cloud Architect
• Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field; Master's degree preferred

Nice to Have:
• Experience in industrial, energy, manufacturing, or aerospace sectors
• Knowledge of OT (Operational Technology) and IoT architecture for industrial applications
• Experience with SAP integration and migration to cloud (SAP on AWS, Azure, or GCP)
• TOGAF or Zachman enterprise architecture framework certification
• Experience with FinOps practices and cloud cost optimization at enterprise scale
• Knowledge of compliance frameworks: SOX, ITAR, NIST 800-53, FedRAMP

Responsibilities:
• Design enterprise-grade cloud architectures for business-critical applications and data platforms across AWS, Azure, and GCP
• Lead cloud migration initiatives, defining target-state architectures and migration roadmaps for legacy systems
• Create detailed architecture documents including solution designs, sequence diagrams, data flow diagrams, and network topologies
• Evaluate and recommend cloud services, tools, and technologies that align with enterprise standards and business requirements
• Establish and enforce cloud architecture governance standards, reference architectures, and design patterns
• Conduct architecture reviews and provide guidance to application development teams on cloud-native design principles
• Collaborate with cybersecurity teams to ensure cloud architectures comply with security policies and regulatory requirements
• Develop proof-of-concept implementations to validate architectural decisions and demonstrate feasibility
• Present architecture strategies and recommendations to business unit CIOs and VP-level stakeholders
• Work with cloud vendors (AWS, Microsoft, Google) and system integrators on strategic technology partnerships
• Mentor and coach junior architects and engineers on cloud architecture best practices
• Drive cloud cost optimization by designing efficient architectures and implementing FinOps governance processes
